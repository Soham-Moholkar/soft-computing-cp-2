FICTIONAL COMMUNITY EQUIPMENT SUPPORT
Synthetic classroom example | Revision 1.0

Eligibility:
Applicants must operate a registered micro-enterprise and reside in the district.
Applicants must submit one application per enterprise.

Exclusions:
Applicants with an overdue public loan are not eligible unless the issuing office has recorded a repayment arrangement.
An incomplete application is not eligible for review until the missing material is supplied.

Documents required:
Submit a copy of the enterprise registration certificate and a current district address proof.
Attach the equipment quotation with the application.

How to apply:
Apply through the district office using the published application form.
