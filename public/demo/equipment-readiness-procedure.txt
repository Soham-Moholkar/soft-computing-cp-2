EQUIPMENT READINESS PROCEDURE
Synthetic operations exercise

The Equipment Register supports the Asset Audit.
The Asset Audit triggers the Service Review.
The Service Review requires the Maintenance Schedule.
The Maintenance Schedule uses the Inspection Calendar.
The Inspection Calendar routes to the Facilities Inbox.
The Facilities Inbox feeds the Daily Brief.
The Daily Brief documents the Shift Roster.
The Shift Roster requires the Coverage Plan.
The Coverage Plan depends on the Escalation Roster.
The Escalation Roster supports the Weekend Desk.
The Weekend Desk verifies the Dispatch Log.
The Dispatch Log might trigger the Service Review.
The Dispatch Log does not support the Daily Brief.
